Numeric tables are easy for computers to multiply and cluster, but human language is messy, ambiguous, and packed with sarcasm and context. **Natural Language Processing (NLP)**—a branch of computational linguistics—bridges that gap so software can parse grammar, extract entities, and hold conversations. In this opening NLP lesson, we trace how early conversational bots created the illusion of intelligence and build our first baseline bot, **Marvin**.

---

## Computational Linguistics, the Turing Test & ELIZA

Every time you dictate a message to your phone or ask a voice assistant for directions, your speech is transcribed to text, parsed for grammatical keywords, and mapped to an executable intent. Back in the **1950s**, **Alan Turing** asked whether a machine could pass a typed conversational test—inspired by *The Imitation Game*—where a human interrogator could not tell whether they were chatting with a person or a computer.

In **1966**, MIT scientist **Joseph Weizenbaum** built **ELIZA**, a simulated psychotherapist that amazed users even though it understood *nothing*. ELIZA simply matched surface patterns and reflected pronouns:

| User Input | ELIZA Pattern Rule | ELIZA Response | Why It Breaks |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| `"I am sad"` | `"I am <X>"` $\rightarrow$ `"How long have you been <X>?"` | `"How long have you been sad?"` | Pure syntax swap; zero emotional comprehension |
| `"You are a bicycle"` | `"You are <X>"` $\rightarrow$ `"How long have I been <X>?"` | `"How long have I been a bicycle?"` | Accepts absurd premises literally |
| Unmatched statement | Fallback generic prompt | `"Please go on."` | Repeats canned responses with no memory |

## Building Marvin: A Baseline Conversational Loop

Unlike ELIZA's hand-written grammar rules, our starter bot **Marvin** uses a minimal 3-step REPL loop: print greeting instructions, read user input until `'bye'`, and return a random conversational prompt. Comparing Marvin's generic replies against ELIZA's keyword reflections immediately reveals what an intelligent bot actually needs: **context tracking**, **sentiment awareness**, and **conversational memory**.
