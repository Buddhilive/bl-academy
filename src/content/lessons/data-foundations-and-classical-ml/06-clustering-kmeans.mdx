## Concept Walkthrough: How K-Means Finds Structure

### The K-Means Loop

Given $k$ initial centroids $\mu_1, \dots, \mu_k$:

1. **Assign**: Each data point $x_i$ joins the cluster whose centroid $\mu_c$ is closest.
2. **Update**: Recompute each centroid $\mu_c$ as the mean of all points currently assigned to cluster $c$.
3. **Repeat**: Stop when centroid positions stabilize or reach max iterations.

K-Means minimizes **inertia**—the Within-Cluster Sum of Squares (WCSS):

$$\text{Inertia} = \sum_{c=1}^{k} \sum_{x_i \in C_c} \|x_i - \mu_c\|^2$$

### Picking $k$: Elbow Method & Silhouette Score

Inertia always drops as $k$ grows (at $k = n$, inertia is $0$). To avoid picking $k$ arbitrarily:

- **Elbow Method**: Plot inertia for $k = 2, 3, \dots, 10$ and look for the bend where adding another cluster yields diminishing returns.
- **Silhouette Score** ($s \in [-1, 1]$): Compares intra-cluster cohesion $a$ against nearest-cluster separation $b$:

$$s = \frac{b - a}{\max(a, b)}$$

Scores closer to $+1$ indicate well-separated, compact clusters.

| Metric | Range | What "Good" Looks Like |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Inertia (`kmeans.inertia_`) | $[0, \infty)$ | Lower is tighter, but pick at the "elbow" |
| Silhouette Score (`silhouette_score`) | $[-1, 1]$ | Higher (closer to `1.0`) means clearer separation |

Run this NumPy snippet to watch K-Means assign points to centroids, update the means, and compute inertia:

X = np.array([
    [1.0, 1.2], [1.4, 0.9], [0.8, 1.5],

# Initial centroids
centroids = np.array([[1.0, 1.0], [6.0, 6.0]])

for step in range(3):
    dists = np.linalg.norm(X[:, None, :] - centroids[None, :, :], axis=2)
    labels = dists.argmin(axis=1)
    inertia = np.sum(np.min(dists, axis=1) ** 2)
    centroids = np.array([X[labels == c].mean(axis=0) for c in range(2)])
    print(f"Step {step + 1} | labels={labels.tolist()} | inertia={inertia:.4f}")

print("Final centroids:\n", np.round(centroids, 3))`}

### 2. Elbow Method & Silhouette Lab in Google Colab

Open the companion notebook to scale audio-style tabular features, sweep $k \in [2, 8]$, and plot both the Elbow curve and Silhouette scores:

<ColabButton notebook="06-clustering-kmeans.ipynb" />

```python
from sklearn.datasets import make_blobs
from sklearn.preprocessing import StandardScaler
from sklearn.cluster import KMeans
from sklearn.metrics import silhouette_score

X, _ = make_blobs(n_samples=300, centers=4, cluster_std=0.8, random_state=42)
X_scaled = StandardScaler().fit_transform(X)

for k in range(2, 7):
    km = KMeans(n_clusters=k, n_init="auto", random_state=42).fit(X_scaled)
    sil = silhouette_score(X_scaled, km.labels_)
    print(f"k={k} | inertia={km.inertia_:6.2f} | silhouette={sil:.3f}")
```
