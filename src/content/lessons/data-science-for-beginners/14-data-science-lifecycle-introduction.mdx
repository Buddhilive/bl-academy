By now you've seen that data science is never just calling a single library function—it is an iterative engineering process. Across industry, that process is organized into five connected stages: **Capturing**, **Processing**, **Analyzing**, **Communicating**, and **Maintaining**. In this lesson, we'll unpack Capturing, Processing, and Maintaining, and compare standard lifecycle frameworks like **CRISP-DM** and **TDSP**.

---

## Stage 1: Capturing (Goals & Data Acquisition)

The capturing stage combines two crucial activities: defining measurable stakeholder goals and acquiring data of sufficient quantity and quality. Before collecting a single row, ask:

- **Problem Alignment**: Has this problem been solved before? What are the time, budget, and compute constraints? What does an acceptable result look like?
- **Data Audit**: Who owns the data? What privacy constraints apply? Is the dataset complete and accurate enough, or should we refine the goal based on what we find?

---

## Stage 2: Processing & Stage 3: Maintaining

During **Processing**, statistical methods and machine learning models (Classification, Clustering, Regression) uncover patterns and predict outcomes. Sitting right between Capturing and Processing is **Maintaining**—the ongoing discipline of storing, cleaning, and securing data throughout the project's lifetime:

| Maintenance Pillar | Key Engineering Decisions |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Storage Topology** | **On-Premise** (owned hardware) vs **Public Cloud** (shared elastic infrastructure) vs **Private Cloud** (strict compliance isolation) |
| **Hot vs. Cold Data** | Keep frequently queried **hot data** on fast SSDs/memory; archive rarely accessed **cold data** to low-cost object storage |
| **Automated Pipelines** | Run consistent cleansing, aggregation, and compression whenever fresh data arrives |
| **Data Security** | Encrypt data at rest/in transit, disclose usage transparently, revoke access when members leave, and restrict write permissions |

---

## Lifecycle Frameworks: TDSP vs. CRISP-DM

Different teams name these stages differently, though the underlying work is identical. **CRISP-DM** (*Cross-Industry Standard Process for Data Mining*) cycles through Business Understanding, Data Understanding, Data Preparation, Modeling, Evaluation, and Deployment. **TDSP** (*Team Data Science Process*) maps those phases into Business Understanding, Data Acquisition & Understanding, Modeling, Deployment, and Customer Acceptance—adding explicit team role definitions for collaborative cloud workflows.
