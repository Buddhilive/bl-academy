Real-world datasets rarely have just one interesting column. Usually you want to know how two or three variables move *together* over time. In this lesson, we'll use **Seaborn**'s relational plotting toolkit (`relplot`, `FacetGrid`, and `lineplot`) on a poignant USDA dataset tracking U.S. **honey production from 1998 to 2012**—spanning the 2006 onset of **Colony Collapse Disorder (CCD)**.

Each row records a state's colony count (`numcol`), yield per colony (`yieldpercol`), total production (`totalprod`), stocks, price per pound (`priceperlb`), and production value (`prodvalue`) for a single year. Putting these columns side by side lets us test supply-and-demand hypotheses visually before writing a single regression equation.

---

## Scatterplots with Color Palettes & Accessible Size Encoding

Using `sns.relplot(x="priceperlb", y="state", data=honey, height=15, aspect=.5)` plots honey prices across every U.S. state. To reveal *how* prices evolved over time, we add a third dimension:

- **Color (`hue="year", palette="YlOrBr"`)**: Uses a warm honey-toned sequential palette showing prices climbing steadily from 1998 (`$0.64/lb` in Arizona) to 2012 (`$1.79/lb`).
- **Size (`size="year"`)**: Maps later years to larger dot circumferences—a great **colorblind-accessible** alternative (or complement) to color alone.

## Line Charts, Confidence Bands, Facet Grids & Dual-Axis Plots

| Seaborn / Matplotlib Tool | What It Reveals in `honey.csv` |
| :--- | :--- |
| `sns.relplot(x="year", y="priceperlb", kind="line")` | Plots mean price per pound with a **95% confidence interval** band (disable with `errorbar=None`), spotlighting a price spike around 2003 |
| **Facet Grid** (`col="year", col_wrap=3`) | Draws 15 side-by-side small multiples comparing `yieldpercol` vs `numcol` for every year |
| **Dual-Axis Plot** (`ax2 = ax.twinx()` + `sns.despine()`) | Shares the `year` x-axis while plotting `# colonies` on the left y-axis and `colony yield` on the right y-axis, showing colony counts stabilizing even as yield per colony declines |

Run this pure-Python analyzer on the Arizona honey production time series (1998–2012) to compare pre-CCD (`1998–2005`) vs post-CCD (`2006–2012`) prices and colony counts.
