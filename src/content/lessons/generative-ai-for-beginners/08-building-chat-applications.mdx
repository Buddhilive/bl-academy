Unlike single-shot text generation, a **chat application** maintains multi-turn state across `system`, `user`, and `model` messages so learners or customers can ask follow-up questions naturally. Earlier rule-based chatbots could only follow rigid decision trees; modern GenAI chat apps combine stateful memory with a well-crafted **System Message**, accessible UX, and continuous quantitative monitoring. In this lesson, we'll design production system messages, measure chat quality with **Perplexity** and **$F_1$**, and compute **Cosine Similarity** across word and news-article embeddings.

---

## The 4-Part System Message Design Framework

Every reliable chat assistant starts with a structured `system_instruction` built from four pillars:

1. **Profile & Boundaries**: Define the target audience, core capabilities, and explicit limitations (e.g., *"You are a medical triage assistant; never prescribe medication and always advise consulting a clinician"*).
2. **Output Format**: Lock structure (Markdown tables, 3-bullet summaries, JSON schemas).
3. **Few-Shot Conversational Examples**: Show 1–2 ideal user/assistant exchanges.
4. **Behavioral Guardrails**: Enforce tone, citation rules, and **4-Dimension Accessibility** (Visual screen-reader clarity, Auditory captions, Motor keyboard navigation, Cognitive plain-language pacing).

## Operational & ML Evaluation Metrics for Chat Apps

Shipping a chat app requires tracking both infrastructure health and model quality:

| Metric | Formula / Definition | What It Catches |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Precision & Recall** | $P = \frac{\text{TP}}{\text{TP}+\text{FP}}, \quad R = \frac{\text{TP}}{\text{TP}+\text{FN}}$ | False alarms vs. missed intents in ticket routing |
| **$F_1$ Score** | $F_1 = \frac{2 \cdot P \cdot R}{P + R}$ | Balanced intent classification accuracy |
| **Perplexity (PPL)** | $\text{PPL}(W) = \exp\!\left(-\frac{1}{N}\sum_{i=1}^{N} \log P(w_i \mid w_{<i})\right)$ | Model uncertainty on domain language (lower is better) |
| **Uptime & Latency** | Availability % and Time-to-First-Token (ms) | Infrastructure degradation and cold starts |
| **CSAT & Anomaly Rate** | Thumbs-up/down ratio and error-rate spikes | Silent quality drift after prompt or model updates |

## Multi-Task Chat & Embeddings in Action

A single chat/embedding SDK (`google-genai`) handles four classic enterprise tasks cleanly:
- **Summarization (`tl;dr`)**: Condensing dense NLP abstracts into one crisp sentence.
- **Zero-Shot Classification**: Routing `"one of the keys on my laptop keyboard broke"` directly to `Hardware Support` among `[Pricing, Hardware Support, Software Support]`.
- **Few-Shot Product Naming**: Generating brand names from seed pairs (`HomeShaker, Fit Shaker` $\rightarrow$ omni-fit adaptable shoes).
- **Semantic Embeddings & Cosine Similarity**: Measuring semantic closeness between words (`automobile` vs. `vehicle` vs. `dinosaur`) and news articles (comparing two soccer transfer stories—Carlos Alberto's move to Werder Bremen and David Beckham's LA Galaxy transfer—against an unrelated human-interest story about Youssif meeting Spider-Man).
